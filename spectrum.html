Good To Know

Bundles & equipment

Bundling options

Spectrum often allows internet to be paired with TV or mobile service. Bundling can simplify billing and may include promotional pricing for a limited introductory period, though terms vary and are set entirely by Spectrum.

Equipment & installation

Plans typically include a modem/router, sometimes with an optional equipment fee. Self-installation is often available, with professional installation offered for an additional cost in many areas.

Pricing and availability disclaimer: Speed tiers, pricing, promotions, and equipment fees described here are general and for orientation only. Listed rates are starting/promotional prices, plus applicable taxes and fees, and may require enrollment in autopay or paperless billing. Installation fees may apply. Offers are typically limited to new residential customers and may change at any time. Everything here is set by Spectrum and can vary by address — please confirm current details directly with Spectrum or through our contact form before making a decision.

Spectrum FAQ

Common questions about Spectrum

Is Spectrum available at my address?+
Spectrum has a broad cable footprint, but availability still depends on your specific address. The best way to confirm is to reach out through our contact form or check directly with Spectrum.
Does Spectrum require a contract?+
Many Spectrum internet plans are offered without a long-term annual contract, though this can depend on the specific plan or promotion. Confirm current contract terms before signing up.
Can I bundle Spectrum internet with TV or mobile?+
In many areas, yes. Spectrum commonly offers bundle options that combine internet with TV and/or mobile service. Availability and pricing for bundles vary by location and current promotions.
What if my speed tier isn't fast enough for my household?+
If you're unsure which tier fits your household, let us know how many people and devices are typically online through our contact form, and we'll point you toward a sensible starting point to ask about.
