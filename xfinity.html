Good To Know

Equipment & add-ons

Whole-home WiFi equipment

Xfinity is known for offering equipment options aimed at whole-home WiFi coverage, which can help in larger homes or homes with weak signal areas. Equipment is often available to rent or purchase, and terms vary by plan.

Bundles & add-ons

Xfinity commonly offers the ability to add streaming packages, mobile lines, or home security features alongside internet service. Availability and current promotional pricing depend on your plan and location.

Pricing and availability disclaimer: Speed tiers, pricing, promotions, and equipment fees described here are general and for orientation only. Listed rates are starting/promotional prices, plus applicable taxes and fees, and promotional pricing may require enrollment in autopay. Xfinity FAQ

Common questions about Xfinity

Is Xfinity available at my address?+
Xfinity has a large cable footprint, especially in urban and suburban areas, but availability still depends on your specific address. Reach out through our contact form and we'll help you confirm the next step.
What is the whole-home WiFi equipment about?+
Xfinity often provides equipment options designed to extend WiFi coverage throughout a larger home. Whether it's included or offered as an add-on depends on the specific plan and any current promotion.
Can I add mobile or streaming services to my plan?+
In many areas, Xfinity allows internet plans to be paired with mobile lines or streaming add-ons. Availability, pricing, and current promotions vary and should be confirmed directly.
How do I know which tier is right for a larger household?+
Let us know roughly how many people and devices are typically online in your home through our contact form, and we'll point you toward a tier range that's worth asking about.
