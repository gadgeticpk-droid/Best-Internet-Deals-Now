Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know before you compare

Is Best Internet Deals Now an internet provider?+
No. We're an independent comparison resource. We organize publicly available information about providers like Spectrum and Xfinity to help you understand your options; we don't provide internet service ourselves.
Are the prices and speeds on this site guaranteed?+
No. Pricing, speeds, equipment, and promotions are set by each provider and can vary by address, plan changes, and time. We recommend confirming current details directly with the provider or through our contact form before making a decision.
Will every plan be available at my address?+
Not necessarily. Cable and internet availability depends heavily on your specific location. The provider pages describe general plan types; actual availability at your address should be confirmed directly.
Does it cost anything to use this site?+
Browsing this site is free. If you choose to sign up for service with a provider, any costs would come from that provider's plans and pricing, not from us.
How do I get help choosing a plan?+
Use the contact section below to send us a few details about your household and what you're looking for, or call the number listed there. We'll point you toward a sensible next step.
